01 / 08StreamLine Spotlight
Slide 5 · Strategy

From “more features” to strategic weight

Problem hook

StreamLine is losing its most valuable subscribers one quiet Reddit search at a time, and without curation, fifteen thousand titles will not save a platform that no one trusts to pick the right one

Value proposition

Spotlight gives StreamLine's fifteen thousand titles a voice, turning an overwhelming catalog into a trusted, editorially guided experience that wins back subscribers who value quality over quantity.

What the PM adds
Strategic priority: no evidence a feature was requested or that it drives revenue or retention.
Low strategic weight: customizable bottle icons/themes look “correct” but have no clear business or user-demand link.
Frameworks changed the output: JTBD, effort/impact, and retention thinking forced usefulness; the AI did not get smarter.
PM role: AI organizes and executes quickly; strategic thinking must come from the PM.
Hypothesis · success metric
41%

Wanderers represent the base with the lowest LTV and highest reliance on trending content.

61%

Trending-content exposure among Wanderers; Spotlight targets the undifferentiated-content problem.

22 pts

Observed churn improvement for Spotlight-exposed Wanderers; target is at least 15 points.

02 / 08Strategic Product Thinking
Slide 6 · Research

The workaround is the evidence

Quality-Sensitive Subscriber

Goal: confidence before spending 20 minutes

Friction: no sense of why something is recommended, which erodes trust over time.

External tools: StreamLineTV, film subreddits, Letterboxd, Rotten Tomatoes/Metacritic, and group chat.

Manual process: see recommendation → search Reddit/Letterboxd → skim 2–3 reviews → cross-check → return to StreamLine or abandon.

Core frustration

The moment they realize they’re doing StreamLine’s job for it — the platform surfaced the title but gave them zero reasoning.

Competitive / workaround insight

The 15,000-title catalog is not the value proposition. Curation with a visible reason is.

Journey map · where Spotlight enters
1 · Open appJamie arrives intending to find something to watch.
2 · Scan generic rowsTrending, Popular, and “Because You Watched X” repeat.
3 · HesitateNothing feels credible or personally relevant. The user looks elsewhere for reasoning.
4 · BounceGoogle, Reddit, a friend, or a competitor app takes the session.

Spotlight inserts a reasoned shortlist exactly at the hesitation point, before the user gives up.

Control · A

Current homepage

Algorithmic rows only, no curated rail. The observed funnel includes a 29-point Homepage-to-Browse drop, 19-point Detail-to-Play drop, and 11% reaching 30+ minute sessions.

Variant · B

Spotlight Curated Rail

3–5 hand-curated titles, above the fold, mobile-first, visually distinct from algorithmic rows. No other product elements change.

Measurement plan

Baseline: not available. The source reports the 22-point relative churn improvement for exposed Wanderers, not standalone non-exposed churn.

MDE: 15 percentage points. Sample size per arm: 166. Significance: p < 0.05.

Isolation: app version, recommendation engine, rows, navigation, onboarding, notifications, billing, and Power User pathways remain identical.

SHIP

Primary metric improves ≥15 points at p<0.05 and the Power User guardrail stays ≥58%.

ITERATE

Direction is positive but lift is below the MDE.

KILL

Primary metric shows no improvement or moves negatively.

Bad signal

High reach + low clicks: content is not compelling. Churn improves + Power User engagement drops: best segment is being harmed. Clicks healthy + churn flat: engagement is not translating into retention.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–3

Build rail, visual treatment, initial curated set, FAQ, dashboard brief, push copy.

Phase 2 · Days 0–90

50/50 launch; weekly guardrails; Day 30 directional check; Day 90 decision.

Phase 3 · Day 91+

If shipped, expand to Casual Browsers and revisit tooling; otherwise redesign or document learnings.

Slide 10 · Story

The aha moment: the reason is the product

Friction → clarity

Writing the one-line “why you will love this” reason forced real clarity on the persona.

The hardest part was resisting the urge to redesign the whole home screen.

Key takeaways
A tight, reasoned shortlist beats a longer list.
Jamie’s trust gap is real, but Wanderers represent the higher-leverage business opportunity.
Spotlight’s value is curation with a visible reason, not simply another algorithmic row.
Next

Close the loop by feeding Spotlight engagement back into the taste model and A/B testing social-proof badges.

PM × AI

AI: organizes and executes quickly. Better frameworks and judgment—not a “smarter” model—created the useful output.
