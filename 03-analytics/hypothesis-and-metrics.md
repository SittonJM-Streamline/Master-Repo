## Pre-work · Hypothesis check
- **Role , who you are solving for (from M2):** The Quality-Sensitive Subscriber
- **Goal , what this user is ultimately trying to achieve:** Find something genuinely worth watching, quickly and with confidence without spending more time deciding what to watch than actually watching it
- **Friction / moment of misery , the specific pain blocking their goal:** The browsing experience offers no trustworthy signal to distinguish a worthwhile title from the noise of 15,000 undifferentiated options  forcing Jamie to either gamble on an unproven pick or abandon the app entirely to find that trust elsewhere.  Jamie opens StreamLine intending to find something to watch. They scroll through the same recurring rows "Trending," "Popular," "Because You Watched X"  for 60–90 seconds. Nothing stands out as credible or personally relevant; every row feels like a guess rather than a recommendation. Rather than risk 20+ minutes on an unproven title, Jamie exits the app to Google, Reddit, or a text to a friend outsourcing the exact job StreamLine was supposed to do.
- **Current workaround , the external tool or manual process they rely on (M2):** Since StreamLine's in-app recommendations fail to earn her trust, Jamie has built her own informal curation system outside the product. She Googles "best shows on StreamLine" before browsing, checks Reddit threads for candid human opinions, texts a friend for a quick recommendation, and maintains a personal watchlist in her Notes app from these external sources. When none of this yields a fast answer, she opens a competitor app instead, resulting in a fully lost session. Together, these workarounds show that Jamie has not abandoned the need for curation. She has simply stopped expecting StreamLine to provide it, and has replaced the platform's discovery function with a patchwork of manual, external alternatives.
- **Problem Hook , your one-sentence framing of the business crisis (M1):** StreamLine is losing its most valuable subscribers one quiet Reddit search at a time, and without curation, fifteen thousand titles will not save a platform that no one trusts to pick the right one
- **Value Proposition , the outcome your initiative promised to deliver (M1):** Spotlight gives StreamLine's fifteen thousand titles a voice, turning an overwhelming catalog into a trusted, editorially guided experience that wins back subscribers who value quality over quantity.

## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** If we extend Spotlight-style curated placements to the Wanderer segment, the 41% of the base showing the lowest curated content exposure (18%) and the highest reliance on trending content (61%), rather than concentrating solely on the actively-researching Casual Browser persona, then we will reduce churn most significantly where the business has the most to gain, since Wanderers already show a 22-point churn improvement under Spotlight exposure, the largest of any segment, because the data reveals that the costliest trust gap is not the vocal, externally-researching user we originally designed for, but the quiet, passively-disengaging majority whose low curated exposure and high trending reliance most closely mirror the undifferentiated-content problem Spotlight was built to solve.
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** The numbers confirm the pain is real and curation helps, but the biggest, most costly leaks (new-subscriber attrition and top-of-funnel drop-off) and the highest-impact segment Wanderers don't match the original persona-driven assumptions, suggesting Spotlight's scope and targeting may need to expand beyond the initial qualitative framing
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Jamie's trust gap at the point of title decision is real and measurably addressed by curation, but it represents a secondary, smaller-scale version of a much larger pattern, one more accurately embodied by the low-frequency, high-trending-reliance Wanderer segment, that the original persona does not fully capture.
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Spotlight removes the friction of undifferentiated, low-trust content discovery, specifically the moment a user reaches a specific title or browsing session without enough curated signal to commit to watching, a pain most acutely experienced by low-engagement Wanderer users (61% trending content exposure) and reflected in the 19-point Detail Page to Play drop-off and the 22-point churn improvement Spotlight already demonstrates for that segment.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** By shifting Wanderers and new subscribers from passive, trending-driven browsing to curated, higher-confidence content selection, Spotlight is expected to reduce early-lifecycle churn, improve session depth, and lift retention specifically within the largest and lowest-LTV segment of the base, translating into a disproportionately high revenue impact relative to the initiative's scope, since it targets a high-volume, currently under-monetized population where even a modest engagement improvement compounds significantly against a base of 41 percent of all subscribers.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** Wanderer Segment Churn Rate, Spotlight-Exposed vs. Non-Exposed

This is the leading indicator because it directly reflects the mechanism the entire hypothesis rests on, curation reducing churn in the segment with the greatest observed sensitivity to it.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Power User Curated Content Engagement Rate (currently 58% curated / 22% trending)

This is the guardrail because Power Users already represent the platform's healthiest, highest-LTV segment ($14.20 avg monthly LTV, 4.8 sessions/week, effectively zero churn), and any shift in Spotlight design aimed at improving Wanderer outcomes must not disrupt what is already working for this base.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** 90 Days (One Full Quarter), Anchored to Two Checkpoints
Thirty days to confirm the signal is alive; ninety days to confirm it's real enough to scale, protected the whole way by a hard guardrail on Power User engagement.
