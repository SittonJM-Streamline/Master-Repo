# Strategic Product Thinking, Module 1 Lab (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **Write one feature from the map that, if an engineer asked “why is this a priority for our 12-week roadmap?”, you honestly could not give a strategic answer for.:** There is no evidence users requested this or that it drives revenue or retention.
- **Write one feature that looks “correct” for the app but has zero strategic weight, you have no idea if it helps the company make money or whether a single user actually asked for it.:** Customizable bottle icons or themes. It looks like a real feature but has no clear link to business goals or user demand.
- **Compare V3 to your Step 1 baseline. What changed, and is the output objectively better, did the AI get smarter, or did your added PM knowledge (JTBD, effort/impact, retention) force it to be useful?:** The AI did not get smarter. Adding PM frameworks like JTBD, effort versus impact, and retention thinking forced better output. The improvement came from human judgment, not the model.
- **Key takeaway: how does this prove the PM’s role is to think while the AI’s role is to execute?:** AI organizes and executes quickly, but strategic thinking must come from the PM. Good frameworks produce good outputs, not better AI.
